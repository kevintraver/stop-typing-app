The Try It tab now shows how close you are to a nudge.

## What changed

- **A meter in Try It.** A bar fills as you type toward the first nudge, with checks for the other two conditions, word breaks and mostly letters, so you can see what's holding it back. After the gentle nudge it switches to the firm nudge or stop window and counts down the time that has to pass first.
- **Try It takes focus.** Opening Settings straight onto the tab puts the cursor in the box, ready to type.

Nothing you type is stored, logged or sent, and automatic update checks stay off unless you turn them on.
