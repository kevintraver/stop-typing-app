You can now dismiss the nudge instead of waiting for it to fade.

## What changed

- **Press Esc** to hide the nudge instantly. You don't need to click it first, and Esc already tells Stop Typing App to drop whatever you were typing from the count.
- **Click the nudge** to dismiss it. Dragging still moves it, and the click that ends a drag won't dismiss it by accident.
- **Hover for a ✕** in the corner of the nudge to close it. VoiceOver users get a Dismiss action too.

Nothing you type is stored, logged or sent, and automatic update checks stay off unless you turn them on.
