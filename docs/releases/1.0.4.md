## What changed

- **Reset your statistics.** Settings → General now shows today's nudge and word counts in a Statistics section, with a Reset Statistics action that starts them over from zero.
- **Homebrew upgrades work.** `brew upgrade` now updates Stop Typing App like any other app. Before, Homebrew skipped it because it assumed the app updated itself.
- **Update checks are explicitly profile-free.** Sparkle's optional system profiling is now switched off outright, not just off by default, and Settings describes exactly what an update check sends: your IP address, the app and updater versions and your language, never your typing.

Nothing you type is stored, logged or sent, and automatic update checks stay off unless you turn them on.
